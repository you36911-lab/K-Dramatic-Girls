K-Dramatic GirlsAbout

Our podcast era

Casual like a late-night chat. Honest enough to go deep.

K-Dramatic Girls is a podcast by two Korean women talking about everyday life, and about everything that everyday life quietly touches: politics, society, identity, and what it means to be Korean today.

Expect honest stories, a little drama, a lot of laughter, and the occasional hot take on culture, belonging, and growing up between worlds.

“Two Korean girls, everyday stories, and the big questions hiding inside them.”
